Kred — Track Money You Lend

A simple ledger for money between friends and family.

Get it on Google Play

What Kred does

Lent someone money and can't remember whether they paid you back? Kred keeps the record. You log what you lent, to whom, and when it's due. As repayments come in — including partial ones — you log those too, and Kred shows you exactly what's still outstanding.

  • Record money you lend, with an optional due date
  • Log partial and full repayments as they happen
  • See who still owes you and how much, at a glance
  • Colour-coded status: active, due soon, overdue, settled
  • Works offline and syncs when you're back online
  • Reminders, per-person summaries, analytics and CSV export on Premium
Kred is not a lender. It does not lend money, charge interest, process payments, or connect you with lenders. It is a private record-keeping tool for money you have already lent to people you know.

Your data

Your records sync to your own private account and nobody else can read them. Your sign-in is stored in your phone's secure keystore. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, under Settings — no email required, no waiting on support.
